Instructional coaching is most useful when evidence from a classroom becomes a focused next conversation. The hard part is rarely the observation itself. It is keeping the record, the focus, and the agreed action in one place that coaches and school leaders can actually use.

**CoachingOS** is software NovaPath builds for that cycle. This is a product description, not a case study. It is not a claim about a named campus, and it is not a scoreboard.

## What the product is for

CoachingOS is designed to help a coaching team:

1. **Capture what was observed** in a form that can be reviewed later.
2. **Name an instructional focus** so the next visit is not a fresh start from memory.
3. **Record a next step** the teacher and coach can both see.

The intended users are people who already have a coaching practice — in district schools and charter networks — and need a shared system around it. The software does not replace a coach’s judgment. It is meant to hold the trail from evidence to action.

## What this page is not

It is not a customer story. We do not use a school’s name here as public proof, and we do not invent adoption figures to make the product sound further along than a conversation can establish.

When a school leader refers a colleague, the useful questions are usually ordinary ones:

- What workflow would this sit inside?
- Who would enter observations, and who would read them?
- How does this support the coaching conversations you already run?

Those questions are a better credibility motion than a list of logos. A referral that starts with the work — not a claim — is easier for another campus to evaluate honestly.
