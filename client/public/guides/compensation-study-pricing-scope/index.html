What determines the cost of a compensation study?

By NovaPath · Published September 11, 2026

On this page
  1. Define the work behind the fee
  2. Compare quotes on equal terms
  3. What to include in an initial inquiry
  4. Does a faster draft change the scope?

NovaPath quotes compensation studies by scope. There is no universal public price: the number and complexity of roles, comparison markets, data preparation, benefits analysis, and deliverables determine the work.

Define the work behind the fee

  • Distinct positions: a headcount of 100 people in five consistent roles is different from 30 people in 25 distinct roles.
  • Role complexity: specialized duties or blended positions may need more judgment and documentation.
  • Markets: multiple locations or comparison groups expand the research and explanation required.
  • Data readiness: incomplete job descriptions, inconsistent units, or missing schedules create preparation work.
  • Compensation elements: benefits and employer contributions add work beyond a base-pay comparison.
  • Deliverables: confirm the report, tables, presentation, review meetings, and revisions that are actually included.

Compare quotes on equal terms

Send every provider the same role list, locations, purpose, and desired outputs. Ask each to state its assumptions, exclusions, revision process, and treatment of scope changes. A lower fee for a narrower analysis is not necessarily a like-for-like comparison.

Distinguish one-time analysis from future updates or implementation support. Ask whether additional source-access costs exist and who is responsible for them. Do not assume ongoing monitoring is included in a single study.

What to include in an initial inquiry

Share your organization type, approximate number of distinct roles, locations, purpose, and decision deadline. You can describe whether current job descriptions and pay schedules are available without uploading employee records to the initial inquiry.

We need a study for [number of roles] across [locations] to support [decision]. We have [available information] and need [deliverables] by [date]. Please confirm scope, information requirements, fee, and review schedule.

Does a faster draft change the scope?

A delivery window does not replace a scope agreement. NovaPath’s published five-business-day draft window starts after agreed scope and confirmation of complete data. Finalization follows consolidated feedback on a separately agreed schedule. Confirm that your project fits the offer before planning around it.

A written proposal is the place to establish the fee and commitments for your engagement. Examples and guides on this site are not binding quotes.
