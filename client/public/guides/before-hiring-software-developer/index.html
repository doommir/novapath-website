Before you hire a software developer: define the work first

By NovaPath · Published September 11, 2026

On this page
  1. Start with one real example
  2. Prepare a brief before comparing providers
  3. Ask for an approach, not just a price
  4. Contact NovaPath while the question is still open

Before you collect developer quotes, describe the task your team needs to complete and where it currently breaks down. A clear workflow makes proposals easier to compare and reduces the chance of buying the wrong solution.

Start with one real example

Choose a recent task: collecting an application, preparing a report, tracking a coaching action, or coordinating a handoff. List who starts it, what information they provide, what happens next, and how the team knows it is complete. Record the exception cases, not just the ideal path.

Replace “we need an app” with a concrete goal such as “staff need one place to see which requests are incomplete and what follow-up is needed.” This illustrative goal leaves room to evaluate an existing tool as well as a custom build.

Prepare a brief before comparing providers

  • The people using the system and the decisions they need to make.
  • The current workflow, including delays and duplicate entry.
  • The information required and where it lives today.
  • Existing tools that must stay in use.
  • The smallest useful first release.
  • A budget range, deadline, and decision owner.

Use fictional or redacted examples during initial discussions. Confirm appropriate handling before sharing personal or confidential records.

Ask for an approach, not just a price

Ask each provider how it will clarify requirements, show a working prototype, test the important workflows, and handle changes. Compare what is included in the quote: design, development, data work, deployment, training, and support may be separate items.

Ask who will control the domain, hosting, accounts, and source code, and how your organization can export its information. Those terms belong in the agreement, not in assumptions made after the build.
