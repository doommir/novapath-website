CoachingOS

Make every coaching conversation build on the last.

CoachingOS is NovaPath’s instructional coaching software for schools and coaching teams. It connects observations, instructional focus, and agreed action steps so coaches and educators can return to the work they started.

Discuss your needs

What CoachingOS supports

  • Capture classroom evidence. Keep observations available for the next coaching conversation.
  • Identify an instructional focus. Use the evidence to decide what deserves attention.
  • Agree on a next step. Make the action clear enough to revisit.
  • Follow through. Bring the previous observation and agreed action into the next visit.

Who is it for?

School leaders, instructional coaches, and teams that want a shared record of coaching work. NovaPath works with the team to understand its instructional model and implementation needs.

How do we get started?

Tell us about your coaching model, the people who will use the platform, and the workflow you want to improve. We’ll discuss scope, implementation, and pricing with you.
