A few details before you start.

When does the five-day timeline begin?

After the engagement scope is agreed and we confirm that all required information is complete. The commitment covers the study draft. Client feedback time, final revisions, and changes to scope are scheduled separately.

What information will you need?

Your job descriptions, salary ranges or compensation information, benefits, service area, and any required comparison criteria. We’ll provide a checklist specific to your study. Please do not submit employee-level records through this inquiry form.

How much does a study cost?

We provide a scoped proposal based on the number and complexity of positions, comparison markets, and reporting needs. Your fee and deliverables are agreed before work begins.

Do I need to schedule a call?

No. Start with the request above and continue by email. If a conversation would help clarify your study, we can arrange one.
