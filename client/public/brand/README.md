# NovaPath brand assets

The NP mark and favicon match the business site at novapath.dev. `np-mark.svg` is a vector recreation of the silhouette supplied by Matt on September 8, 2026, not an original vector master. The favicon places the mark on cream for legibility.

The NP mark retains brand purple #54128A. The education page uses deep plum #302230, warm white #F8F6F1, and muted gold #DAC8A3, with Georgia serif headings and Geist Sans body text. Geist is self-hosted in ../fonts with its license.
