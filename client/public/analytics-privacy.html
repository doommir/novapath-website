Website analytics

Updated September 12, 2026

With your permission, we use Google Analytics to understand visits to our public website and count successful inquiries. This notice covers that optional analytics feature.

Your choice

Google Analytics stays off until you choose “Allow analytics.” You can decline or change your choice at any time using “Analytics choices” at the bottom of the page. The site works either way. We remember your choice in this browser for up to six months, separately on each domain.

What Google receives

When you allow analytics, Google receives page visits, time and device/browser information, a cookie-based identifier, the referring website, and selected actions such as a successful inquiry. Google processes network information to deliver the service. We use these reports to improve our website.

Our tracking sends public page paths without URL query strings or fragments. It does not send form answers, names, email addresses, website-chat messages, generated designs, or private preview links. Advertising personalization and Google signals are off.

Turning analytics off

Declining stops this tracking and removes this site's Google Analytics cookies. We also keep analytics off when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Browser blocking tools may prevent analytics even after you allow it.

For Google's processing details, see How Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.
