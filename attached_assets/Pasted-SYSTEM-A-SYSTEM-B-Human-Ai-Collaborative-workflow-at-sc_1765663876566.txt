SYSTEM A + SYSTEM B (Human+ Ai Collaborative workflow at scale) 
Preventative Control Infrastructure for Schools
System A captures what schools are doing. 
System B predicts what will break — and tells them what to do next.
Together, they let schools prevent failure instead of reacting to it.

The Problem
Schools already have dashboards.
Dashboards explain the past.
They do nothing to prevent what’s coming next.
By the time problems show up in:
test scores
state dashboards
accountability reviews
leaders are already late, public, and expensive.
There is no system in education that answers:
“If nothing changes, what breaks next — and how do we stop it early?”



The Solution
A closed-loop control system.
System A — Measurement & Action (Coaching Operating System and Dashboards)
Lives inside schools
Used daily by all instructional staff
Captures observations, scores, workflows
Executes human decisions
Status: Live in 8 schools across 3 organizations.

System B — Intelligence & Prediction
No teacher UI
No raw text
No PII
No student data
System B learns from System A’s signals and answers:
“What happens next — when — and what single move prevents failure?”

How It Works
Coaches work normally in System A
Signals are distilled (no raw data leaves)
System B models trajectory and inaction risk
System B computes the next-best move
That recommendation is sent back into System A
Measure → Predict → Suggest → Act → Learn
No new tools.
No extra dashboards.

What Coaches See
Inside their existing dashboard:
Suggested Next Move
Schedule one follow-up within 10 days
Impact: ~40% reduction in regression risk
System B suggests.
Humans decide.

Why This Wins
Most edtech:
describes the past
judges people
waits for outcomes to fail
System A + B:
predict failure before it’s visible
recommend small, low-friction actions
route guidance to the point of control
prevent problems quietly
This is preventative control, not analytics.

Defensibility
One intelligence engine
Many dashboard systems feeding it
Each treated as a separate measurement instrument
Clean-room architecture (no raw text, no PII)
Cross-org learning without data pooling
The moat is longitudinal, real-world coaching behavior — normalized by design.

What This Is Not
Teacher evaluation
Test-score analytics
State dashboard replication
AI replacing humans
No labels.
No blame.
No waiting for failure.

One-Line Summary
System A measures and executes.
System B predicts and guides.
Together, they prevent failure instead of explaining it.
