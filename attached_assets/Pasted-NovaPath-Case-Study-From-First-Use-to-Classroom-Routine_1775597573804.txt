Across every classroom, one pattern kept showing up:

Students can answer the question.
They struggle to support their answer with evidence.

This wasn’t isolated to a grade, a teacher, or a rubric.

It was consistent across the entire system.

Why That Matters

Most tools tell you if students are struggling.

NovaPath shows you where and how they are struggling — fast enough to adjust instruction the next day.

Instead of:

“Writing scores are low”

Teachers get:

“Students are not citing evidence clearly”
“Explanations are underdeveloped”
“Responses stop at the claim”

That level of specificity changes what happens in the next lesson.
