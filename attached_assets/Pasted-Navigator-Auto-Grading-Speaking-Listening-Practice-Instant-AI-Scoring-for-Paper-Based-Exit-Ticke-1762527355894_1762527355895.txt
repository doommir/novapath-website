Navigator Auto-Grading + Speaking & Listening Practice
Instant AI Scoring for Paper-Based Exit Tickets
The Problem
Teachers spend hours grading short written responses and have limited time to give personalized feedback or support oral fluency practice. Students complete work on paper, but insights stay locked there.
The Solution
Navigator’s custom web app transforms paper responses into real-time feedback using OCR, Whisper, and LLM-powered rubric scoring.


Students write, snap a photo, read their response aloud, and receive instant, scaffolded feedback aligned to standards
How It Works
Student completes exit ticket 
Student takes a photo 
OCR extracts text from handwriting.
Student reads response aloud 
Whisper transcribes and evaluates 
LLM applies rubric, scoring written 
AI provides targeted feedback 
Score Pushed to Google Sheets 




Rubric Scoring Framework
Category
4 – Exceeding
3 – Demonstrating
2 – Progressing
1 – Developing
Restate the Question
Clearly & fluidly restates question, citing title/section
Accurately restates in full sentence
Attempts but may omit/reorder
Partially or inaccurately restates
Answer the Question
Correct, complete, insightful
Correct and complete
Partially correct
Incorrect or missing
Cite Evidence
Smoothly integrates accurate quotes
Refers explicitly with accurate quotes
Some explicit but unclear
No or unrelated evidence
Elaborate
Thorough, deep explanation
Logical, relevant explanation
Weak or minimal reasoning
Missing or unclear reasoning

Final Score: Average of category levels → Exceeding / Demonstrating / Progressing / Developing
Whisper Modifier: Speaking & listening clarity and fluency adjust final score ±0.25

Feedback Layer
“You restated accurately and chose good evidence. Next: explain why that quote proves your point — start with ‘This shows that …’.”
AI feedback follows rubric-aligned stems and guides next-step revisions instantly.

Tech Stack
OCR: Google Vision (handwriting extraction)


Speaking & Listening: OpenAI Whisper (fluency + pronunciation)


LLM: GPT-4o-mini (rubric scoring + feedback)


App Backend: Supabase


Frontend: React web app optimized for iPads
