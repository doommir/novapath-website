## DESIGN QUALITY PASS

The current page looks vibe-coded. Every section has the same purple dashed borders, inconsistent spacing, and no visual rhythm. I want it to look like a polished, professional consulting site — closer to the HexaCore template (hexacore.framer.website) in terms of quality and cohesion.

### Global design changes:

**Kill the purple dashed borders.** Every card on the page currently has a `border: 1px dashed` or similar purple outline. This is the single biggest thing making it look AI-generated. Replace with:
- Cards should use a subtle solid border (`1px solid rgba(124, 92, 255, 0.15)`) or no border at all
- Use background color differentiation instead: card backgrounds at `#1A1425` against page background `#0F0A1A`
- On hover, cards can get a subtle glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(124, 92, 255, 0.1)`
- The "featured" or "Most Popular" pricing card can have a brighter border (`1px solid #7C5CFF`)

**Consistent section spacing.** Every major section should have the same vertical padding: `padding: 96px 0` on desktop, `padding: 64px 0` on mobile. Right now some sections are cramped and others have huge gaps.

**Section dividers.** Between major sections, use either:
- A subtle gradient line (like HexaCore's curved gradient accents)
- OR consistent spacing with no divider (cleaner)
- Do NOT use thick purple borders or heavy horizontal rules

**Typography hierarchy.** 
- Section eyebrow labels (like "SERVICES", "WHAT WE'RE HEARING"): 12px, uppercase, letter-spacing 2px, color #7C5CFF, font-weight 600
- Section headlines: 36-42px on desktop, 28-32px on mobile, font-weight 700, white
- Section subheads: 16-18px, color #B4B0C4, max-width 600px, centered
- Card titles: 20px, font-weight 600, white
- Card body text: 15px, color #B4B0C4, line-height 1.6
- "Best for" or tag text on service cards: 13px, color #A78BFA, italic or with a small icon

**Smooth scroll.** Add `scroll-behavior: smooth` to the html element. All nav anchor links should smoothly scroll to their sections.
