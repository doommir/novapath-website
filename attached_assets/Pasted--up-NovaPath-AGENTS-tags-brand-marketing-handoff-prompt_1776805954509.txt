You're writing marketing copy for NovaPath, a two-person AI consultancy run by Dan Whitlock (CEO, K-12 track) and Matt Varner (COO, SMB/sprint track). You are not writing a brand voice from scratch. The voice is already established. Your job is to translate it into the ~8 pages of the marketing website without losing what makes it work.

2. **Design system** — `/Users/mattvarner/Desktop/NovaPath Design System/` — ivory paper (#F7F3EC), deep forest green (#2D4A3E) accent, warm ink (#1A1A1A) body, Fraunces (display) + Geist Sans (body) + JetBrains Mono (code/meta). Editorial posture. No gradients. Rules over shadows. Read `colors_and_type.css` and the existing `ui_kits/marketing/` components so the copy matches the visual register.

### Hard rails (do not violate)

1. **No hallucinated case studies.** The v1 design system invented "Lincoln County USD," "Tilden & Reyes LLP," and "Western Regional BOCES." All three are fake. Delete them. The only real clients you may reference, and only as general categories until permission is cleared, are:
   - **CrewFit** (Ben's fitness studio — SMB software build)
   - **Concord** (Gabriela + Ruth — financial services platform)
   - **Jim Zimmerman** (RMS property-tech build, HouseCanary integration)
   - **Scott Ravenscroft** (Flutter code review / paid diagnostic)
   - **Anthoby** (personal shopper SMB)
   - **Andi Abbot / Heartland** (SMB)
   
   If you're not sure a case study is safe to name, write the slot as `[CASE STUDY PENDING — GET CLIENT PERMISSION]` and move on. Do not invent substitutes.

2. **No hallucinated geography, dates, or institutional affiliations.** NovaPath is based in Murrieta, California. Dan travels. There is no "Santa Fe office," no "Cooperative," no board, no advisory panel. Est. 2024 is fine. "Murrieta, CA & remote across North America" is fine. That's the extent of verifiable geography copy.

3. **No fabricated quotes.** If a testimonial block is needed, write it as `[PULL QUOTE — COLLECT FROM CLIENT]`. Do not write a plausible-sounding quote and attribute it to a real client. Do not write an anonymous quote and imply it's real.

4. **No fake metrics.** "40% faster," "3x engagement," "$2M saved" — not without a source. If a stat-shaped claim would strengthen the copy, write it as `[METRIC PENDING]` and note what the claim would need to cite.

### Banned words (rewrite if they appear)

leverage, empower, unlock, journey, synergy, transformation partner, AI-powered, solutions (as in "AI solutions"), boutique, curated, bespoke, disruptive, revolutionary, game-changing, ecosystem, cutting-edge, world-class, best-in-class, next-gen, seamless (unless literally describing a seam)

### Required vocabulary (use these)

**Cobuild** (verb — the signature term, must appear in hero and /approach)
**Sprint** (the SMB unit of work — two-week timebox)
**Ship** (not "deliver," not "roll out")
**Tool** (not "solution," not "platform")
**Working prototype** (not "MVP")
**Your team / your operators** (who you build with)
**Field notes** (the writing section name)
**Handoff** (the thing competitors don't actually do)
**Teacher-in-the-loop** (for K-12 copy)
**Your stack, your repo, your call** (sprint tagline — canon)

### Voice attributes (check drafts against these)

- Direct, not clipped. Warm and short.
- Specific, not general. "A 4-person law firm" > "small businesses."
- Confident, not boastful. "We ship a working prototype in week one" > "leading AI consultancy."
- Opinionated. Takes in public, plainly stated.
- Dry humor about the *industry*, never about *clients*. Roast Gartner, never the superintendent.
- First-person plural throughout. "We," not "NovaPath."
- Contractions. "We don't," not "we do not."

### Register by track

NovaPath has two tracks. Same brand, different rooms. Do not merge them.

- **SMB / Sprint track** (Matt's domain) — operational, numbered, timebox-and-price forward. Think: "Two weeks. Your stack. Friday demo. Done." Reads like Matt wrote it.
- **K-12 track** (Dan's domain) — philosophical, outcomes-forward, relational. Think: "Your teachers shape the tool. Your board signs the deck. Your district owns the code." Reads like Dan wrote it.

If a paragraph could sit in either track without editing, it's too generic. Push it to one side.

### Pages to draft

Produce copy for all eight, in this order. For each page, deliver:
- Page title (tab + H1)
- Meta description (≤160 chars)
- Hero (headline + subhead + primary CTA + secondary CTA)
- Section-by-section body copy
- Any microcopy (button labels, link text, form placeholders)

---

#### 1. Home (`/`)

The single most important page. Must accomplish:
- Tell a visitor in 5 seconds what you do and who you do it for
- Surface both tracks without burying one
- Include one real pull quote slot (mark as `[PULL QUOTE — COLLECT]` for now)
- Link to /approach, /sprints, /k12, /work, /field-notes
- End with a dead-simple contact CTA

Hero headline direction: lead with **"We cobuild AI with the people who'll use it."** Support with a subhead that names the two audiences (K-12 districts + founders).

#### 2. Approach (`/approach`)

The philosophy page. Explain cobuilding. Explain what you're NOT (no retainers, no black boxes, no 180-page decks). Use the brand voice doc's "anti-consulting posture" list. Define "sprint" and "handoff" as real terms with real meanings. This page is where the quietly brave copy lives — consider using a softened version of "our expertise is not development, our expertise is sitting next to people who know their operation better than we do."

#### 3. Sprints (`/sprints`)

SMB services page. Price forward. Table or structured list with:
- Scoping call — 60 min — free
- Diagnostic / assessment — [PRICE PENDING] — 1 week
- Sprint build — $1,500–$2,500 — 2 weeks
- Extended build — custom — 4–8 weeks
- Office hours (post-ship) — 4 weeks — included

Include what a sprint week actually looks like (day-by-day). Use Matt's operational register. End with one real case study callout.

#### 4. K-12 (`/k12`)

Dan's page. Different hero, same brand. Think: readiness assessment, teacher-in-the-loop pilot design, procurement & policy review (FERPA, state, district), ship a working pilot. Copy needs to feel like it was written by someone who respects educators and has sat in a faculty lounge. End with `[CASE STUDY PENDING]` slot.

#### 5. Work (`/work`)

Real case studies. For v1, write three cards as `[CASE STUDY PENDING — CrewFit / Concord / Jim Zimmerman]` with structural placeholders (challenge, approach, outcome). Do NOT invent the content. Do include a section header and intro paragraph. This page exists for SEO and credibility even when empty.

#### 6. Field Notes (`/field-notes`)

The writing section. This page is an index, not an essay. Write:
- Section intro (2–3 sentences on why you write this)
- Two placeholder post cards:
  - "What wedge-and-expand actually looks like with a law firm" — Matt
  - "Why K-12 AI RFPs fail before they're written" — Dan
- Each card: title, byline, 1-sentence dek, published date slot, read time slot

#### 7. About (`/about`)

Two-person firm. Be honest. Photos of Dan and Matt. Short bios — 4–6 sentences each. Matt: technical operator, pattern-recognizer, operational backbone. Dan: strategist, K-12 domain expert, relationship-first. No invented credentials. If Dan has real K-12 consulting history, reference it in general terms only.

#### 8. Contact (`/contact`)

Not a form. Two email addresses (placeholder `hello@novapath.co` and `dan@novapath.co` — confirm with Matt before publishing), a Calendly link (placeholder), and a "here's what to include when you reach out" soft gate: (1) what problem you're trying to solve, (2) what you've already tried, (3) your timeline. That's the entire page. Resist adding more.

### Deliverable format

Return the result as a single markdown document with one `# H1` per page and clean H2/H3 structure inside. Every block of body copy should be in plain prose — no lorem ipsum, no placeholder text except the explicit `[CASE STUDY PENDING]` / `[METRIC PENDING]` / `[PULL QUOTE — COLLECT]` / `[PRICE PENDING]` flags noted above.

### One more thing

The brand voice doc contains a Straussian read at the bottom — for Matt's eyes only. Do not surface any of that in public copy. The site's job is to be consistent with that psychology, not to expose it.

When in doubt: be shorter, be warmer, be more specific, be more honest.
