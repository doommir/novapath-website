“How It Works” Section

Title: “How Human Moments Become Intelligent Workflows”

Text:

Every NovaPath automation begins with people:

Human Inquiry: A teacher initiates a workflow (attendance, check-in, alert).

AI Insight: NovaPath drafts or analyzes, surfacing next steps.

Human Reflection: Staff review, adjust, and approve.

No automation is final until a human approves.

Title: “Built on Human-Centered AI Principles”

Principle	Short Description
