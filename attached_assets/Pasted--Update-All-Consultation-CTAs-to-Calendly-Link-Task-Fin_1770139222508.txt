# Update All Consultation CTAs to Calendly Link

## Task
Find and update every consultation/booking CTA on the consulting page to point to a Calendly booking link. Every button, link, or CTA that invites visitors to schedule a call, book a consultation, or talk to Dan should point to the same URL.

## Target URL
```
https://calendly.com/daniel-whitlock/30min
```

All links should open in a new tab (`target="_blank"` with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`).

## Locations to Update

### 1. Navigation Bar
The "Schedule a Consultation" button in the top-right nav. Update its `href` to the Calendly link.

### 2. Hero Section
The "Schedule a Free Consultation" button in the hero area. Update its `href` to the Calendly link.

### 3. Contact Form Section ("Let's Talk About Your District")
This is the form at the bottom of the page with fields for name, email, district, title, and a message. Two options here — pick whichever is easier:

**Option A (preferred):** Keep the form but add a line directly above it:
```
Prefer to book directly? Schedule a free 30-minute call →
```
Style "Schedule a free 30-minute call →" as a purple text link (`#7C5CFF`), pointing to the Calendly URL. This way visitors can either fill out the form OR book directly — both paths work.

**Option B:** Replace the entire form with a centered CTA that links to Calendly. Only do this if the form isn't actually connected to anything (no backend, no email notifications). If the form works and sends Dan submissions, keep it and go with Option A.

### 4. Any Other CTAs
Search the entire codebase for any other buttons, links, or text that reference booking, scheduling, consultations, or "let's talk." Common patterns to search for:
- `Schedule`
- `Consultation`
- `Book`
- `Let's talk`
- `Contact`

- Any `href` pointing to `#contact`, `#book`, `/contact`, or similar anchor/page links

Update all of them to the Calendly URL.

Check what this button currently does. If it scrolls to a booking section or contact form, leave it as-is (it's serving a different purpose — showing methodology). Only update it if it currently points to a booking/scheduling action.
